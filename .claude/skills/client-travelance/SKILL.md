## 3. Business overview

- Canadian travel insurance provider for visitors to Canada, Canadians travelling outside their province, international
  students, and students/children needing accident coverage.

### Products to promote (priority order)
1. **Visitors to Canada Insurance (VTC):** emergency medical cover for visitors and Super Visa applicants.
2. **Travel Insurance (TRIPs):** travel medical, trip cancellation/interruption, baggage and related cover for Canadians travelling.
3. **International Student Insurance (ISP):** health/travel insurance for international students in Canada.

### Target locations
Alberta, Ontario, British Columbia, Manitoba, Saskatchewan (Canada).

## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** visitors to Canada needing emergency medical insurance; Super Visa applicants (parents and grandparents);
  international students; Canadians travelling outside their province or country.
- **Secondary:** parents and grandparents visiting Canada; Super Visa applicants and families; international students'
  spouses/dependents; working holiday participants; Canadian families travelling internationally.
- **Exclude:** free travel insurance seekers; insurance jobs/careers; insurance courses, training or certifications;
  people looking for insurance companies to work for; home, auto, life or business insurance searches.
- **Pain points (use in ads):** high medical costs in Canada for uninsured visitors; Super Visa requires qualifying
  medical insurance; unexpected illness or injury for visitors and students.
