### Corporate Team Building campaign
- Traffic reaches the dedicated Team Building page, but many users book standard escape rooms instead of higher-value
  corporate packages. Only a few bookings came directly from the corporate package.
- Client: corporate groups may find premium packages ($560 to $900) expensive.
- Actions: keep optimizing; build a dedicated Team Building landing page for A/B testing; analyze search terms and add
  negatives.

### Birthday Party campaign
- Birthday package bookings fell sharply despite traffic and conversions. Users land on birthday pages but book regular
  escape rooms.
- Decisions: create a dedicated Birthday Party Search campaign; build a dedicated birthday landing page with minimal
  navigation; A/B test package-level conversion rate. Priority because birthday season starts around September.

### Competitor campaign
- Dedicated competitor campaigns targeting VR competitors were launched. Performance inconsistent and weaker than core
  campaigns.
- Plan: competitor landing page showing Zero Latency/FLEE advantages; keep checking ad copy, search terms, behaviour.

### Keywords and search terms
- Converting keywords named: "Escape room team building", "Team building activities near me".
- Full search-term reports requested for corporate campaigns; client will mark relevant and irrelevant terms.
- Negative keyword expansion ongoing across campaigns.

### Conversion tracking and technical issues
- Several purchases close together make keyword attribution hard.
- UTM parameters and tracking info do not pass consistently through the booking process.
- Google Ads form submissions differ from dashboard-reported conversions.
- Conversion setup changes need verifying in production.
- Booking flow issue: users sometimes return to the beginning after selecting an escape room.
- Actions: audit conversion tracking; verify production deployment; investigate the booking-flow issue.

### Landing page strategy
- Planned pages: 1. Birthday Party, 2. Team Building, 3. Competitor campaign, 4. Jumanji (December launch).
- Goals: keep visitors on the intended offer; reduce leakage to other booking pages; A/B test; integrate booking flow
  into the page.

### Budget and scaling (agreed)
- Do not increase budgets aggressively all at once.
- Monitor changes for 10 to 12 days before major budget decisions.
- Raise gradually on top campaigns, using campaign-level revenue and ROAS.

### Seasonal

- Jumanji: dedicated landing page and promo campaign before the December release.

### Overall client direction
Client is satisfied with Google Ads and sees Google as the strongest platform. Priorities: 1. scale budget with ROAS
