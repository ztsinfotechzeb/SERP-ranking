## 3. Business overview (from questionnaire)

Questionnaire doc: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SNPcCfql_YBn288PkjjbBS0mXEbtjr2iMoSKWY1kpa0/edit?tab=t.0

- Full-service access solution provider. Matting for rent and sale, equipment and crews for installation and removal,
  a mat washer to clean and bleach mats, and full logistics to and from customer sites.
- Traditional bolted wooden access mats suit about 90% of projects. Steel frame rig mats and wooden crane mats for
  heavier equipment and loads. Rubber access mats do not get damaged by tracked equipment, while wooden mats do, and
  renters pay for wooden mat damage at project end.
- Client claim: "largest fleet of A, B and C grade bolted wooden access mats for sale in Canada". Do not use "largest"
  in ads unless the client gives proof (golden rule 5).
- Client wants to **push mat sales** this year. Renting is offered for companies that do not want to spend capital.
  Client says renting is a 100% tax write-off while a purchase is depreciated. Use tax wording in ads only after the
  client approves the exact text.
- Best seller: **8' x 14' wooden access mats**.
- Customer list: 500-1,000 contacts.
- Promotion today: sales/business development team (3 salespeople, 2 managers) and an SEO company for 5 years.
  Previous ad spend 2,500/month with returns not tracked.
- 99% of projects are won through bidding; the best price quoted wins.
- No promotions. Has run **demo days** (link on the website).
- Traffic drops sharply in **December** (holidays).
- No resellers or marketplaces.
- **Avoid the "TerraLam" brand name** (a manufacturer they no longer use or associate with). Add it as a negative and never use it in copy.
- Conversion tracking before us: none.

## 4. Audience

- **Industries (questionnaire):** oil and gas, pipeline, power line transmission and distribution, civil construction,
  mining, renewable energy, forestry, emergency response.
- **Interest:** getting across muddy, wet or unstable terrain.
- **Primary:** construction companies, infrastructure contractors, pipeline operators and energy businesses that need
  temporary access mats and ground protection for heavy equipment.
- **Secondary:** businesses that support, supply or manage construction and industrial projects; companies wanting to
  recover value from previously bought mats (buy-back).
- **Exclude:** homeowners, DIY customers, small residential contractors, general construction materials or unrelated
  equipment, permanent road construction, indoor flooring, consumer-grade ground protection, organizations outside
  GFI's delivery network.
