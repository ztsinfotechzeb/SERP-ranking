## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** friends and groups seeking unique entertainment; families and teenagers; couples / date night;
  birthday and celebration groups; corporate teams and companies.
- **Secondary:** gamers and VR enthusiasts; tourists and visitors; school/youth groups; event planners;
  adventure and puzzle enthusiasts.
- **Exclude:** users outside the service area; very young children unsuited to the experiences; users seeking
  non-interactive entertainment; job seekers; low-intent audiences with no event/entertainment need.
- **Pain points (use in copy):** finding unique group entertainment; wanting something beyond traditional
  outings; hard to plan memorable group celebrations; few interactive team-building options; desire for
  immersive, exciting experiences.
- Live Halloween ad sets use two audiences: **Friends** and **Family**.
