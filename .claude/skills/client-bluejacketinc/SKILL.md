## 3. Business overview (from questionnaire)

- Oldest manufacturer of model kits in the USA (established 1905). Specialized in ship and boat models.
- USP in the client's words: customer service and build help for modellers, "the best kits on the market".
  Do not use "best" in ads without proof (golden rule 5). Use "Since 1905" and "100+ years" instead.
- Ideal customers: model makers, mostly **age 40+**. Retired or affluent people (60+) who build a kit over
  1-2 years and may later resell the finished model or sell it at auction.
- Customer list: about 5,900 emails/phones. Use it for Customer Match and Shopping/PMax audience signals.
- Promotion today: website/online marketing. No paid ads before us. No offline sales.
- Traffic peak: **Christmas**. Plan budget and promos for Nov-Dec.
- No regular promotions or discounts.
- Also sells wholesale to other shops (resellers).
- **Exclude: gift shop items** (client request).

## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** model ship builders; model boat/ship kit buyers; ship-modeling hobbyists; people looking for
  ship-model fittings and parts; people looking for model shipbuilding tools and supplies; custom ship
  model buyers; people who need model restoration.
- **Secondary:** hobbyists and DIY enthusiasts, scale-model enthusiasts, collectors, museums and historical
  organizations, hobby shops and specialty retailers, nautical enthusiasts, nautical gift buyers.
- **Exclude:** full-size boat/ship shoppers, boat transport, real ship parts or marine equipment, boat repair
  or maintenance, boating/sailing/fishing gear, **free model ship plans or downloads**.
- **Negative themes:** the above plus "free", "plans pdf", "download", full-size boat terms, marine supply,
  and gift shop items. The live list is in the Ads detail overview working doc.
- **Pain points (use in ads):** hard to find kits with accurate historical detail; hard to source specific
  fittings, parts and accessories; no reliable supplier for rigging and specialist tools; hard to find
  correct scale-specific components.
