## 3. Business overview (from questionnaire)

- Australian e-bike brand, operating nationwide for **11 years**. Street-legal e-bikes for commuting, leisure,
  adventure and everyday mobility.
- Range includes commuter, step-through, folding and fat-tyre models. Features: Samsung batteries, low-step designs,
  suspension, long range, 25 km/h street-legal assistance.
- Sells through a **nationwide dealer network** (not mainly direct online). Dealers offer test rides, spare parts and
  warranty service. This is why the conversion goal is an external link click (to dealers/store), not a purchase.
- Models: BOLT, ONYX, CRUZE, NOMAD, POGO, URBAN.
- ONYX: client said in June that ONYX sales were zero for that month (normally about 100 a year; 72 YTD at the time)
  and she wants it pushed on Meta, Google and SEO.

### Products to promote (priority order)
1. BOLT: practical everyday commuter
2. ONYX: comfortable city and lifestyle riding
3. NOMAD: adventure and off-road
4. POGO: compact and versatile
5. URBAN: everyday urban commuting

(CRUZE is in the range but not in the priority list.)

### Target locations
Sheet: **VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS**. NSW excluded for now (dealers cannot sell Rilu bikes for a couple of months under a
new compliance/certification rule). Focus extra effort on QLD and WA. For WA, client suggested Perth plus 50 to 100 km.
Keep supporting VIC and SA (performing well).

## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** e-bike commuters; urban and city riders; eco-conscious consumers; leisure riders; adults looking for a
  car alternative.
- **Secondary:** adventure and off-road riders; fitness and cycling fans; older riders wanting assisted mobility;
  students and local commuters; delivery and utility riders.
- **Exclude:** users outside Australia; traditional bicycle-only shoppers; people not interested in electric
  mobility; job seekers; low-intent audiences.
- **Pain points:** high fuel and commuting costs; traffic and parking; need for convenient everyday transport; limited
  affordable mobility; wish for eco-friendly transport.
