# Elevated Luxury Studio (tab `ELS`): dated history

Source: Master Profile row 5 of the `ELS` tab (snapshot 07-Oct-2026), columns `Last Optimization (Last 3 Months)`,
`Last Client Update or Comments or Feedback (Last 3 Months)`, `Our Reply / Action Taken` and `Client Complaint / Issue`.
The tab has no update-log rows (row 6+). Year is 2026 for all dates. Where the sheet gives only a month, the entry
says so. "We" = Zebra Techies (owner Tamasa Kundu). "Client" = Tarryn Els.

## June: setup and launch

- **June (no day given), setup.** We got access to GA4, GTM, Merchant Center and Shopify and reviewed the setup.
  Page view, add-to-cart and begin-checkout were tracking through Shopify. A COD test order was planned to verify
  purchase tracking. We removed/paused the old test PMax campaign the client had built before we started.
- **June (no day given), research.** Competitor and keyword research. Keyword groups: Dresses, Tailored Shirting,
  Outerwear, Knitwear, Skirts. Ad copy, sitelinks and callouts built.
- **June, client feedback on copy.** We fixed a 32-character headline, typos and "outerwear" naming, and moved the copy
  to her brand language (tailored, refined, timeless, designed in Sydney). Dress keywords narrowed to the single
  dress then in stock. Outerwear and Knitwear copy added for winter.
- **18-Jun (06.18.2026).** Contract start date in sheet. Monthly cycle on the 18th.
- **25-Jun.** Search campaign launched, Dresses and Shirting first. Budget A$1,000/month. Australia-wide targeting
  instead of four cities, so budget was not spread thin and Google could learn faster.

## July: Shopping, keywords, first complaints

- **Before 3-Jul.** Corrected Merchant Center product data (wrong colour images, category mismatches).
- **3-Jul.** Standard Shopping launched with product-only images.
- **July (no day given).** Added search-term keywords such as cashmere sweater, silk skirt, knitted maxi dress. Added
  irrelevant and competitor terms as negatives. After her feedback: dropped Australian-unfriendly terms and broad terms
  like "midi skirt", shifted weight to dresses and outerwear for winter, researched button-up / button-down shirt
  keywords after she said the shirts were not really "tailored".
- **July to August.** Client's top complaint: conversion tracking. Purchase not clearly primary, duplicate and inactive
  Purchase actions, "Needs attention" and "Misconfigured" flags, conversions inflated by page views and cart events.
- **22-Jul.** Client: reports are too optimistic and activity-focused. Wants purchases, revenue, cost per purchase,
  ROAS, Shopping vs Demand Gen split.
- **23-Jul.** We sent a detailed response document.

## August: tracking fixes, PMax dispute

- **13-Aug.** We agreed to a more commercial report format (spend, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, conversion value,
  ROAS). We shared screenshots showing Purchase was primary.
- **17-Aug.** Our message said PMax would be held (per her instruction).
- **18-Aug.** We argued that zero GA4 key events did not mean Google Ads tracking was broken. A Performance Max build
  was started the same day, against her hold instruction.
- **19-Aug.** Client objected to the PMax build. We first said it was a Shopping PMax prepared internally with nothing
  launched; later admitted it had briefly served (about A$14, 1,116 impressions) before being paused. Agreed to hold
  it. Still on hold. The "Clickbait" image flag was cleared for later review.
- **20-Aug.** Deleted the inactive "Purchase (Website)" conversion action.
- **21-Aug.** Client: never add competitor or aspirational brands as negatives; check with her first. Copy should say
  "designed in Australia", not "made in". Asked for a spring shift and a US expansion outline. We said we would
  double-check the wording and asked her for a product-focus calendar. The US outline was never sent.
- **25-Aug.** Imported the GA4 purchase event into Google Ads.
- **26-Aug.** COD test order #1017; purchase event fired in GA4 Realtime.
- **Late August (deadline).** Client asked for an add-to-cart drop-off diagnosis by end of August. We said we would
  collect more data; no final diagnosis is in the thread.
- **August (client figure).** Blended MER about 0.68. We kept Maximise Conversions while purchase data built up and
  deferred Target ROAS.
- **31-Aug.** Client asked to concentrate the about A$30/day budget and pause Search.

## September: restructure, friction, notice

- **2-Sep.** Search paused at client request. Demand Gen launched at $20/day; Shopping reduced to $10/day. Demand Gen:
  static images, her approved copy, warm remarketing audiences merged into one group (small lists), prospecting
  layer. She approved the split. YouTube off until she supplies video.
- **4-Sep.** Client said she never asked for Gmail to be excluded and wanted it on. We said Gmail was already included
  in both ad groups (impressions were minimal at first, 3). Gmail added back to prospecting on 4-Sep (per optimization
  notes). Fixed the Shopping campaign's conversion goal so it was no longer "Eligible (Limited)". A purchase of about
  $302 happened this day.
- **8-Sep.** We asked her to confirm the 4-Sep purchase.
- **9-Sep.** Client: the 4-Sep purchase was organic, not paid; do not count it as a Google Ads purchase. No recorded
  reply on removing it. Page View and View Item set to Secondary. Add to Cart and Begin Checkout stay Primary with her
  agreement.
- **September onward.** Negatives from live search terms: gift cards, skorts, off-brand labels.
- **18-Sep.** Removed "Alpaca Jumper" from negatives after she objected.
- **21-Sep.** Client: finding bad keywords (alpaca, IE Clothing) and managing negatives is our job, should be proactive
  on a regular cadence. We said negative management is our responsibility and was shared only for her overview, based
  on real search terms; defended IE Clothing as a legitimate label. No review cadence agreed. We agreed to report only
  her chosen metrics (including GA4 purchase and Shopping vs Demand Gen split). Confirmation Received Date 09.21.2026.
- **22-Sep.** Removed "Harris Tapper" from negatives. Client repeated: no brand negatives without asking. Also "nothing
  new added this month" (PMax, video, new additions on hold).
- **23 to 25-Sep.** We asked three times whether she would allow GTM to be reinstalled on the site. No reply.
- **27-Sep.** Client gave notice. Reason: spend "isn't delivering a return that justifies it"; "I am making no money
  with Google." Her Shopify order-level attribution showed no orders from paid Google clicks in Aug to Sep, with sales
  reconciling to organic search. About A$1.7k spent with almost no purchases.
- **28, 29, 30-Sep and 1-Oct.** We asked for more time and followed up. No direct answer on notice, billing or campaign
  continuity.
- **30-Sep.** Client confirmed the contract ends at the end of the payment cycle, 18-Oct. We explained how Meta and
  Google attribution differ for a later order.
