# Client Communication

Channels: Basecamp (default for formal updates), WhatsApp / Telegram (quick updates), email, meetings
(bi-weekly or monthly). Use the channel the client last used unless the message is formal (reports,
approvals, complaints: also post in Basecamp/email for a written record).

Tone: polite, professional, concise, specific. Use the client's first name. Never blame the client.
No jargon without a one-line explanation. Response time target: within 4 business hours; complaints within 2.

Every message in and out gets logged (Channel + client message + our reply) in the project tab.

## Welcome and questionnaire
```
Subject: Welcome to {Agency}: Getting started with your {Google/Meta} Ads

Dear {Client name},

Thank you for choosing {Agency} to manage your {platform} advertising. I am {Employee}, your account
manager for {Project}.

To build campaigns that bring you the right enquiries, please:
1. Complete the short service questionnaire: {link} (about 15 minutes)
2. Grant the access below (step-by-step guide attached):
   • {Google Ads: accept our manager (MCC) request / Meta: add our Business Manager as partner}
   • Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Business Profile, Search Console
   • {Merchant Center / CRM / CMS if applicable}

Once received, we will share keyword research for your approval within {x} working days.

Kind regards,
{Employee}
{Agency}
```

## Answering a performance question
Structure: acknowledge → facts (numbers, period) → reason → action + timeline → offer a call.

## Complaint flow
1. Acknowledge within 2 business hours, thank them, no defensiveness:
   ```
   Hi {name}, thank you for flagging this. I understand the concern about {issue}. I am reviewing the
   account right now and will share the findings and our action plan by {time/date}.
   ```
2. Investigate (search terms, tracking, lead log, changes in the log, spend).
3. Reply with: what happened (root cause), what we have already fixed, what we will do next with dates,
   how we will report back. Offer a call.
4. Sheet: `Client Complaint / Issue`, `Complaint Status` = Open → In Progress → Resolved;
   `Risk Status` Amber/Red + reason; action owner + due date. Tell the responsible employee immediately.
5. Follow up after the fix date with results.

## Budget / billing

