# Service Questionnaire (send to every new client)

Map each answer to the Master Sheet column shown in [brackets].

## A. Business overview [Business Overview / Service Related Questionnaire]
1. Business name, website, years in business, locations/branches.
2. Describe your business in 2–3 sentences.
3. Average order / job value and profit margin (range is fine).
4. Business hours; do you take calls outside them?
5. Seasonality: busiest and slowest months.

## B. Services [Services / Products to Promote (Priority)]
6. List all services/products. Which 3 should we prioritize and why?
7. Any services you do NOT want to advertise?
8. Pricing or packages we can mention? Current offers?

## C. Locations [Target Locations]
9. Areas you serve (cities, postcodes, radius). Areas to exclude?

## D. Audience [Primary / Secondary / Excluded Audiences, Jobs To Be Done, Use Cases, Pain Points]
10. Who is your ideal customer (B2C/B2B, age, role, industry, property type...)?
11. Other customer types you serve?
12. Who do you NOT want enquiries from?
13. What is the customer trying to get done when they contact you?
14. Typical situations that make someone search for you (e.g. moving out, inspection, emergency)?
15. Top problems/frustrations customers have before they find you?
16. Common questions or objections before buying?

## E. Keywords & negatives [Negative Terms That Exclude]
17. Words customers use to describe what you do?
18. Terms/searches you do NOT want to appear for (jobs, DIY, free, specific services)?

## F. Competition & positioning [Competitive Landscape, Differentiation, Proof Points or USPs]
19. Top 3–5 competitors (names/websites).
20. Why do customers choose you over them?
21. Proof: number of reviews & rating, awards, certifications, insurance, guarantees, years, clients served.

## G. Goals & tracking [Conversion Goal, Current Conversion Tracking Set Up Details]
22. What counts as a lead/sale (call, form, WhatsApp, booking, purchase)?
23. Target cost per lead or ROAS; how many leads per month do you need?
24. Lead-to-customer close rate?
25. CRM used? Can we get a user login / lead status updates?
26. Any existing tracking (GA4, GTM, call tracking, Pixel)? Who manages the website?

## H. Budget & history [Monthly Budget, Daily Budget, Last Optimization]
27. Monthly ad budget.
28. Previous ad experience: what worked, what didn't? Access to old accounts?

## I. Creative [Media Doc]
29. Logo, brand colours, fonts, photos, videos, testimonials: please share or give a Drive link.
30. Tone of voice preferred (premium, friendly, urgent, corporate)?

## J. Communication [Meeting Frequency, Communication Channel]
31. Preferred channel (Basecamp, WhatsApp, Telegram, email) and main contact person.
32. Preferred meeting frequency (bi-weekly / monthly) and time.
