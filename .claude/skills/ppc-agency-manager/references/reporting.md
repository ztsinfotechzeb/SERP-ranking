# Reporting Templates

Pull numbers from the platforms for the exact period. State the currency. Compare with the previous period and
the target. After sending, log a row with the metrics and the `Report / File Sent Link`.

## 1. Daily brief (every working day, via the client's preferred channel)

```
Hi {Client first name},

Quick update on {Project} for {DD-MMM-YYYY}:

Work done today
• {e.g. Added 14 negative keywords from search terms (jobs, DIY, free quotes outside area)}
• {e.g. Added 3 new converting search terms as exact-match keywords in "Deep Cleaning"}
• {e.g. Replaced 2 low-performing headlines}

Yesterday at a glance: {Spend} spend | {Clicks} clicks | {Conversions} leads | CPL {CPL}

Next: {one line}

Regards,
{Responsible employee}
```
Keep it under 120 words. No numbers you cannot verify.

## 2. Weekly performance report (fixed weekday, SAME Google Sheet every week)

Weekly sheet structure (one row per week appended at the bottom; never create a new file):

| Week (Mon–Sun) | Campaign | Spend | Impr. | Clicks | CTR | Avg CPC | Conversions | Conv. Rate | CPL/CPA | Calls | Forms | WhatsApp | Qualified Leads | ROAS/Value | Impr. Share | Top Search Terms | Work Done | Next Week Plan |

Add a total row per week and WoW % change. Then message:

```
Hi {Client first name},

The weekly performance report for {Project} ({period}) is updated in the same sheet: {link}

Summary
• Leads: {x} ({+/-y%} vs last week) | CPL: {currency}{z} (target {t})
• Spend: {currency}{s} of {currency}{budget} weekly budget
• Highlight: {best performing campaign/keyword}
• Challenge: {issue + what we are doing}

Work done this week: {3 bullets}
Plan for next week: {2–3 bullets}

Happy to discuss anything on our next call.

Regards,
{Responsible employee}
```

## 3. Weekly conversion report (every Friday)

Fill the project's `Conversion Reporting Format` sheet, one row per lead:

| Date & Time | Source (Google/Meta) | Campaign | Keyword/Ad | Type (Call/Form/WhatsApp/Chat) | Name | Phone/Email (masked in emails) | Call Duration | Call Recording Link | Form/Message Summary | Lead Status (client) | Quality (Qualified / Not qualified / Spam / Duplicate) | Notes |

Then message:
```
Hi {Client first name},

Please find this week's conversion report ({period}): {link}

• Total leads: {n} (Calls {c} | Forms {f} | WhatsApp {w})
• Call recordings are linked in the sheet for your review.
• {k} leads look spam/duplicate; we have {action: added negatives / captcha / excluded placements}.

Could you please mark the lead status (converted / not converted) in the sheet? This helps us optimise the
campaigns toward the enquiries that actually become business for you.

Have a great weekend,
{Responsible employee}
```

## 4. Monthly consolidated report (Monthly Reporting Folder)

Sections (Google Slides/Docs or Sheet dashboard, filed as `{Project} - Monthly Report - {Mon YYYY}`):
1. Executive summary: 3 lines (result vs goal, reason, next month focus).
2. KPI table: this month vs last month vs target: spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions by type,
   qualified leads, CPL/CPA, conv. rate, ROAS (if ecommerce), impression share.
3. Trend chart (daily or weekly leads and CPL).
4. Campaign / ad group / top keywords & search terms; Meta: campaign, ad set, top creatives.
5. Device, location, day/hour insights.
6. Competitor & auction insights.
7. Landing page / CRO observations.
8. Work done this month (from the log rows: Optimization, Daily Update).
9. Client feedback received & how we acted.
10. Recommendations & plan for next month (with budget impact), approvals needed.

After sending: refresh Row 5 rolling 90-day summaries and Risk Status.
