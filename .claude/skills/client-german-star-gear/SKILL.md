## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** automatic vehicle owners with transmission issues; German and European car owners; luxury and
  premium vehicle owners; high-mileage vehicle owners; fleet and commercial operators; automotive workshops and garages.
- **Secondary:** used-car owners; car enthusiasts; dealerships; truck and bus owners; previous customers and
  website visitors (remarketing).
- **Exclude:** users outside the UAE; non-vehicle owners; job seekers; users seeking unrelated car services;
  low-intent audiences.
- **Pain points (use in ads):** expensive transmission replacement; gear shifting problems, jerking and slipping;
  no reliable transmission specialist; unclear repair costs and diagnosis; long vehicle downtime.
