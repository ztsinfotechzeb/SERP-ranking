## 3. Business overview (from questionnaire)

- Better Fit is a **personalized fitness app**. It builds workouts from the user's goals, fitness level,
  preferences and available equipment.
- Features: personalized weekly workouts, activity dashboard, workout builder, exercise guidance, progress
  tracking, performance insights.
- For users who want to build muscle, lose fat, gain strength, or train consistently at the gym or at home.
- Positioning: a structured, personalized digital fitness coach that removes guesswork and helps users stay
  consistent.
- Revenue comes from **memberships/subscriptions**. Several package tiers exist.
- Promote: **Sign Up**.
- Target locations: **NZ and AUS** (meeting: major cities incl. Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane).

## 4. Audience

- **Primary:** gym-goers and fitness enthusiasts; people wanting to lose fat or build muscle; beginners needing
  structured workouts; home and gym workout users; people seeking personalized plans.
- **Secondary:** strength-training enthusiasts; people focused on consistency and progress tracking; people
  returning to fitness; busy professionals needing flexible workouts; personal-training alternative seekers.
- **Exclude:** users with no fitness goals; professional athletes seeking advanced coaching; users outside target
  markets; job seekers; low-intent audiences.
- Negative terms: na (not used on Meta).
- **Pain points (use in ads):** hard to keep a consistent routine; no personalized plan; unsure which exercises to
  do; hard to track progress; low motivation.

## 5. Competition & positioning

- Landscape: fitness and workout apps, online personal trainers, gym-based personal training, YouTube fitness
  programs, generic workout-plan platforms.
- Differentiation: workouts personalized to goals; plans adapted to level and equipment; structured guidance;
  progress and performance tracking; works at home and at the gym.
- Proof points (features only): personalized weekly plans, Workout Builder, progress and activity tracking,
  exercise guidance and performance insights, supports muscle gain, fat loss and strength, flexible workouts.
  No ratings, user counts or results claims in the sheet. Avoid health-outcome promises (Meta policy).
- Auction insights / competitor ads: not in sheet.
